I welcome enquiries from motivated doctoral, master's, and undergraduate students whose interests align with quantitative finance, insurance, applied mathematics, and statistical machine learning.

## Available research directions

- Carbon markets, emissions trading systems, and carbon-credit risk
- Statistical arbitrage, pairs trading, and market microstructure
- Insurance-linked investment and stochastic insurance models
- Sustainable pension-fund investment and portfolio decarbonization
- Statistical machine learning, explainable AI, and financial risk analytics
- Monte Carlo simulation, stochastic processes, and computational finance

## What to include in an enquiry

Please email [quantfilab@gmail.com](mailto:quantfilab@gmail.com) with a short introduction, your CV or academic background, the topic that interests you, and any relevant mathematical, statistical, or programming experience.
