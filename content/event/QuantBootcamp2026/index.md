## Quant Bootcamp is coming

QuantCU and QuantCorner invite students, early-career professionals, and everyone interested in quantitative finance to build essential knowledge and complete practical projects using real data from **efin.finance**.

The programme combines online learning, live sessions, coding practice, an onsite session, and hands-on quantitative projects. It is designed for a cohort of up to **500 participants**.

## Twelve learning modules

1. **Introduction to Quant** — Understand quantitative-finance roles and the work performed across the industry.
2. **Markets, Instruments, and Financial Statements** — Build a practical understanding of financial markets, instruments, and company information.
3. **Python for Quant Work** — Use Python to analyse data and develop programs for quantitative work.
4. **Factor Models** — Construct, evaluate, and select factors for investment models.
5. **Statistics for Financial Data** — Apply statistical methods to financial datasets.
6. **Return and Risk** — Measure investment performance and manage risk.
7. **Time-Series Models** — Analyse and forecast observations recorded sequentially through time.
8. **Backtesting without Overfitting** — Test strategies correctly while reducing research and model-selection errors.
9. **Statistical Arbitrage** — Study trading strategies built from statistical relationships.
10. **E-Finance Toolkits** — Use professional tools and financial data for analysis.
11. **AI and LLMs in the Research Process** — Apply artificial intelligence and large language models responsibly within quantitative research workflows.
12. **Quant Careers and Industry Panel** — Meet industry professionals during the onsite session and learn about career paths and real working experience.

## Hands-on quantitative projects

The programme concludes with practical projects that guide participants through the full research cycle: framing a question, preparing and analysing data, developing a quantitative idea, and testing an investment strategy independently.
