## Keynote lecture

**Quantitative Finance in the Age of Digital Innovation**

Dr. Pasin Marupanthorn will deliver the keynote lecture at the Digital Innovation and Financial Technology Conference (DIFT 2026-2), organized by the International College of Digital Innovation, Chiang Mai University.

The lecture introduces quantitative finance to a broad audience and discusses how mathematical modelling, data, computation, artificial intelligence, and systematic decision methods are influencing modern financial innovation.
