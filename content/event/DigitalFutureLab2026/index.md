## About the activity

Digital Future Lab 2026 was a one-day workshop designed to help participants understand digital finance through practical learning and explore how artificial intelligence can extend new ideas into opportunities for modern businesses.

The programme brought together speakers from quantitative finance, digital assets, investment strategy, education, and online financial communities. Dr. Pasin Marupanthorn joined the speaker group in his role as Vice President of the Thai Association of Quantitative Analysts and Financial Engineers (TQF).

## Event information

- **Date:** 19 September 2026
- **Time:** 08:30–16:30
- **Venue:** Lifelong Learning Conference Room, Faculty of Economics, Chiang Mai University
- **Format:** One-day workshop and discussion
- **Focus:** Digital finance, artificial intelligence, practical learning, and new-economy business opportunities
